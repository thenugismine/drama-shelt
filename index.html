My watch diary

A shelf of stories, rated my way.

This collection began with my K Dramas spreadsheet. It brings together the dramas I’ve watched, the ones on my list, my favorites, and my movie ratings.

Grades are shown exactly as I gave them. Extra plus signs mean extra enthusiasm; they haven’t been converted into numerical scores. Scores out of 10 and written reviews are added separately.

Titles with more than one spreadsheet entry keep their earlier grades and statuses in their details. The latest sheet entry supplies the displayed grade and status.
